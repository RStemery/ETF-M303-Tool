Model Transparency
  • Assumes the past 30-90 trading days of price movement is a useful signal, and scores each ETF only relative to the other ETFs in the same run.
  • Uses unadjusted closing prices from Alpha Vantage's free tier, so dividends are left out. Data may be stale, so the "data as of" date is always shown.
  • A high rank means strong recent performance under your settings. It does not mean the ETF will keep rising or is the best investment for you.
  • Not financial advice. The model ignores fees, valuation, taxes, and what you already own.
